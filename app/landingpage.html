10 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW.

Do first responders or paramedics need an app to scan my badge?

add
No. Any standard smartphone camera (Apple iOS or Android) opens your emergency triage pass in under 1.8 seconds directly in the mobile browser with zero downloads, zero user accounts, and zero passwords required.

How is my private medical history protected against unauthorized scans?

add
LifeQR implements strict dual-tier data segregation: a public scan reveals only vital resuscitation information (blood group, fatal allergies, active medications, emergency contacts). Sensitive longitudinal medical history, lab files, and doctor notes remain strictly encrypted behind authenticated physician logins.

What happens if I lose my printed card or wristband?

add
You have total control. Log in to your Patient Dashboard and click "Revoke & Rotate QR Code" to instantly generate a brand-new cryptographic token and immediately invalidate the lost physical card.

Is LifeQR completely free for patients and families?

add
Yes. LifeQR emergency identity profiles, printable QR badges, digital wallet passes, and emergency contact alerts are 100% free forever for every patient and family.

How does the 1-tap live GPS emergency SOS beacon work?

add
When you press the SOS beacon in your patient dashboard, LifeQR transmits your live GPS coordinates, blood type, and critical allergies directly to regional ambulance dispatchers over Socket.IO while automatically alerting your registered emergency contacts with your live location.
