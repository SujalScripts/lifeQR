09 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do responders need an app to scan my badge?

add
No. Any standard smartphone camera opens your emergency triage pass in under 1.8 seconds directly in the mobile browser with zero downloads and zero passwords required.

How is my medical history protected?

add
A public scan reveals only vital resuscitation information (blood group, fatal allergies, active medications, emergency contacts). Detailed records remain encrypted behind authenticated physician logins.

What happens if I lose my card?

add
Log in to your Patient Dashboard and click "Revoke & Rotate QR Code" to instantly generate a brand-new cryptographic token and immediately invalidate the lost physical card.

Is LifeQR free for families?

add
Yes. LifeQR emergency identity profiles, printable QR badges, digital wallet passes, and emergency contact alerts are 100% free forever for every patient and family.

add
Pressing the SOS beacon transmits your live GPS coordinates directly to regional ambulance dispatchers over Socket.IO while automatically alerting your registered emergency contacts.
